About Implant Dentures Albany

Implant Dentures Albany is a dental practice on North Pearl Street in downtown Albany, dedicated to implant-supported tooth replacement.

Patients come to us for several reasons: a denture that no longer fits, the loss of most of their natural teeth, or a desire to move from removable teeth to something more permanent.

We explain the differences between snap-in and fixed designs, how many implants each needs and what recovery looks like. We also discuss affordable ways to plan treatment.

The office is open Monday to Saturday from 8 am to 5 pm, and we accept cash and credit cards.
